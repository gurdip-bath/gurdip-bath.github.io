Contractor Hub

Contractor Management System · Dataverse Solution

Dataverse · MDA · BPF · Power Automate · JS

Problem

Contractor intake, certifications, and contract status lived across spreadsheets and email. Coordinators manually chased teams when new contractors were onboarded. Compliance gaps — expired contracts, missing contact details, untagged skills — were only caught late.

Solution

  • 13-table Dataverse model with junction table for contractor certifications
  • Two-stage BPF to guide intake and trigger Power Automate on stage advance
  • JavaScript web resources for on-form compliance warnings before assignment

Architecture

  • End-to-end Dataverse data model — 13 custom tables, auto-number IDs, lookups, and junction table
  • Two-stage Business Process Flow with Power Automate on BPF stage advance
  • Notification flow — Outlook email, reason capture popup, and Dataverse write-back
  • Split sitemap for Contractor Management and Project Delivery personas
  • Three JavaScript web resources for certification logic and contract risk warnings
  • Three custom security roles and managed solution packaging

Outcomes

Outlook / Power Automate

Power Automate

Notify-team flow & stakeholder email · 4 screens

Step 1 of 4

Reason Capture Popup

  • Popup prompts the user for the reason a contractor is being onboarded
  • Captured text becomes the body of the automated Outlook notification
  • Reason is written back to Dataverse for auditability and traceability
  • Ensures context travels with the notification — not just a blank system alert

Step 2 of 4

BPF Success Confirmation

  • BPF confirms the automation ran successfully before the user moves on
  • Clear success state reduces uncertainty about whether the team was notified
  • Failed flows surface in the process — not silently missed in the background
  • Closes the intake loop with visible confirmation in the app

Step 3 of 4

Team Email Delivered

  • Outlook 365 delivers the notification to the team inbox automatically
  • No manual email drafting or distribution lists to maintain
  • Email includes the user-entered reason from the intake popup
  • BPF stage advance triggers the notify-team flow — no manual chasing required

Step 4 of 4

Power Automate Flow

  • Cloud flow triggered from BPF stage advance in the model-driven app
  • Sends Outlook email, captures reason, and writes back to Dataverse
  • Scoped error handling and idempotency checks for reliable production behaviour
  • Packaged in a managed solution for DEV → PROD promotion via ALM

Learnings

  • BPF-triggered flows need clear stage criteria — documenting advance conditions early avoids silent failures
  • On-form JS warnings caught compliance issues faster than post-save validation rules alone
  • Split sitemap by persona reduced clutter for intake vs delivery users
